# Contentgen compliance-by-design

Contentgen should use only documented APIs, licensed/authorized data sources, and user-authorized account connections. No bypassing platform restrictions, scraping behind authentication, copying other creators' media, or storing social access tokens in the mobile client.

AI-generated media must be labeled where a platform requires/supports it. Generated visuals should be original and must not deliberately reproduce logos, copyrighted characters, living-artist styles, or a real person's likeness without appropriate rights/consent.

Trend signals are inspiration, not permission to copy a trending post. Contentgen should generate a new concept rather than download/repost another creator's work.

Factual claims sourced from trends/news should retain source provenance and go to review when verification is missing. High-risk content is blocked from automated publishing. Sponsored/brand content must use the platform's required disclosure controls.

Publishing must follow each platform's current developer terms, app-review/audit requirements, user authorization, privacy choices, rate limits and content policies. Legal requirements vary by market; this file is an engineering policy, not legal advice.
